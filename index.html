    WELCOME ABOARD

    A tap is all it takes.

    Tap anywhere in the flight area to give Medy a lift. Keep a steady rhythm to fly through the openings in the blue gates.

    • Clear a gate to earn one point.
    • Collect gold coins for your flight log. These are game coins with no cash or loyalty value.
    • Explore seven destinations: Malta, Rome, Milan, Athens, Madrid, Düsseldorf, and Paris. Start in Malta; every five gates takes you to the next city.
    • Find your rhythm: fly through tighter openings that narrow as you progress. Every ten gates raises your speed multiplier. Anticipate 18 patterns, including stair climbs, double waves, switchbacks, pulses, and high or low shelves. One collision ends the flight.
    • On desktop: Space or ↑ to flap. P or Escape to pause.
